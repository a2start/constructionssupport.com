About The Course

What Is The Health, Safety & Awareness Course?

The Health, Safety & Awareness course is the recognised online training route to a CSCS Green Card. It's designed for anyone new to construction who doesn't yet hold an NVQ, covering the core health and safety knowledge you need before working on site.

The course is completed entirely online, so you can work through it at your own pace and don't need to take a day off work. You'll receive a certificate as soon as you finish, which you can then use to apply for your CSCS Green Card.
