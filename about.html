About Construction Helps

Supporting The UK Construction Workforce

We're an independent booking agent helping construction workers pass their CITB test and get the right card, faster.

Our Story

Why We Started Construction Helps

Booking a CITB test and figuring out which CSCS, Skill or JIB card you need can be confusing, especially if you're new to the industry. Construction Helps was built to make that process simple — one clear booking journey, honest guidance, and a real support team behind it.

We work with construction workers, tradespeople, apprentices and managers across the UK, helping them book tests at over 150 approved centres and apply for the right card the first time.
