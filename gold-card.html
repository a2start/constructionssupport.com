CSCS Gold Card

For advanced craftspeople and supervisors with Level 3 qualifications.

About This Card

What Is The Gold Card?

The CSCS Gold Card recognises two groups: Advanced Craftspeople who hold an NVQ/SVQ Level 3 in their trade, and Supervisors who combine a Level 3 qualification with recognised supervisory training.

Gold Card holders are typically the most experienced hands-on tradespeople on site, often leading small teams or taking responsibility for the quality and safety of a work area.

Who It's For

Is This The Right Card For You?

  • ✓Experienced tradespeople with an NVQ/SVQ Level 3
  • ✓Site supervisors responsible for a team or work area
  • ✓Workers progressing from a Blue Card into a senior craft role
Requirements

What You'll Need To Apply

  • ✓Hold an NVQ/SVQ Level 3 (Advanced Craft) or a supervisory qualification alongside a Level 3
  • ✓Pass the CITB Operatives or Supervisors HS&E test as applicable
  • ✓Provide evidence of your qualification and, for supervisors, your role on site
