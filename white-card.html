CSCS White Card

For degree-level professionals working towards full construction NVQs.

About This Card

What Is The White Card?

The White Card is a temporary, non-renewable card for people who hold a relevant degree, professional qualification or membership, but who have not yet completed a full construction NVQ.

It covers Academically Qualified Persons (AQP), Professionally Qualified Persons (PQP), and specialist categories such as ACRIB refrigerant handlers, giving qualified professionals time-limited site access while their NVQ is finalised.

Card Types

White Card Sub-Types

The White Card covers a few different situations — here's how to tell them apart.

Academically Qualified Person (AQP)

Holders of a relevant construction-related degree.

Professionally Qualified Person (PQP)

Members of a recognised professional body in construction.

ACRIB Refrigerant Handler

Specialist card for registered refrigerant handling technicians.

Requirements

What You'll Need To Apply

  • ✓Pass the CITB Managers & Professionals or Operatives HS&E test as applicable
