Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Terms & ConditionsPrivacy PolicyRefund PolicyDisclaimerFAQs

Last updated: 19 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Construction Helps collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use this website or our booking services, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address and phone number
  • Booking details, including your preferred test centre, date and card type
  • Postcode information, when you use our test centre finder to calculate distances
  • Payment information, processed securely by our payment provider — we do not store full card details
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages visited, to help us improve the website

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To book your CITB test and process your card application
  • To communicate with you about your booking or application status
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To improve our website and services

4. Test Centre Finder And Postcode Lookups

When you search for your nearest test centre, your postcode is sent to the postcodes.io open data service to calculate an approximate location. We do not store the postcodes you search with, and this data is used only to calculate distance to our test centre network.

5. Sharing Your Information

We share the minimum information necessary with CITB, CSCS, JIB, ECS, CPCS, test centres or scheme bodies where required to complete your booking or card application. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at Info@constructionhelps.com, by phone on 0800 002 5614, or by post at 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, United Kingdom, WC2H 9JQ.

Construction Helps is your independent booking partner for CITB tests, CSCS, Skill and JIB cards, and online construction e-learning across the United Kingdom.
