Card Comparison

CSCS Cards Explained

Compare all six CSCS card colours to find the right one for your qualifications and experience.

Green Card — Labourer

The starting point for anyone new to construction site work.

Blue Card — Skilled Worker

For qualified tradespeople who have proven their skills with an NVQ Level 2.

Gold Card — Advanced Craft / Supervisor

For advanced craftspeople and supervisors with Level 3 qualifications.

Red Card — Trainee / Provisional / Apprentice

Temporary cards for people who are training or new to the industry.

White Card — Academically / Professionally Qualified

For degree-level professionals working towards full construction NVQs.

Black Card — Manager

The senior card for construction managers and directors.
