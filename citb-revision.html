Key Topics

What The CITB Test Covers

The CITB test draws its questions from a fixed set of topics. Here are the areas most candidates need to revise.

📘

General Responsibilities

Your legal duties, reporting hazards and who is responsible for safety on site.

📘

Accidents & First Aid

Reporting accidents, near misses and where to find first aid on site.

📘

Health & Welfare

Site welfare facilities, noise, manual handling and occupational health.

📘

PPE

Selecting, wearing and maintaining personal protective equipment correctly.

📘

Fire Prevention

Fire risks, extinguisher types and evacuation procedures.

📘

Working At Height

Ladders, scaffolding, harnesses and fall prevention basics.
