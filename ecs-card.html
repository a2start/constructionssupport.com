About The ECS Card

What Is An ECS Card?

The Electrotechnical Certification Scheme (ECS) is the official certification system for electrotechnical workers in the UK. It verifies qualifications, experience and competency in various electrical-related roles.

Holding an ECS card proves your skills, identity and adherence to industry standards, and is widely recognised and requested across UK construction and building services sites.
