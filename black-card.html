CSCS Black Card

The senior card for construction managers and directors.

About This Card

What Is The Black Card?

The CSCS Black Card is held by construction managers who have achieved an NVQ/SVQ Level 4, 5 or higher, or an equivalent management qualification recognised by CSCS.

It confirms that the holder has the knowledge and experience to manage people, resources, quality and safety across a construction project.
