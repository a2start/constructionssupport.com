CSCS Red Card

Temporary cards for people who are training or new to the industry.

About This Card

What Is The Red Card?

Red Cards cover several temporary categories for people who are part-way through their construction career: trainees enrolled on an NVQ, apprentices, provisional card holders who are new to the industry, students on an industry placement, and experienced workers awaiting a formal assessment.

Because there are several Red Card types, it's important to apply for the one that matches your exact circumstances — our team will help you choose the right one.

Card Types

Red Card Sub-Types

The Red Card covers a few different situations — here's how to tell them apart.

Trainee

For workers registered on an NVQ/SVQ who have not yet completed it.

Apprentice

For those enrolled on a recognised construction apprenticeship.

Provisional

For new entrants who have passed the test but not yet enrolled on an NVQ.

Industry Placement

For students undertaking an industry placement as part of a course.

Experienced Worker

For experienced workers awaiting a formal on-site assessment.

Requirements

What You'll Need To Apply

  • ✓Pass the relevant CITB Health, Safety & Environment test
  • ✓Provide evidence of enrolment on an NVQ, apprenticeship or placement (where applicable)
  • ✓Apply for the correct Red Card sub-type for your situation
