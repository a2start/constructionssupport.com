About The Test

What Is The CITB Test?

The CITB Health, Safety & Environment (HS&E) test, sometimes called the touch-screen test, is a multiple-choice exam that every applicant must pass before they can apply for a CSCS, Skill or JIB card.

The test takes around 45 minutes and covers 50 questions on general health and safety topics, plus a behavioural case study section. You need 45 out of 50 correct to pass.
