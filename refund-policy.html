Refund Policy

How cancellations, rescheduling and refunds work when you book with us.

Terms & ConditionsPrivacy PolicyRefund PolicyDisclaimerFAQs

Last updated: 19 July 2026

1. Booking Fees

When you book a CITB test or submit a card application through Construction Helps, the fee you pay covers the relevant exam, application or course fee, our booking fee, and VAT where applicable.

2. Free Rescheduling

We offer free rescheduling of your CITB test booking, subject to availability at your chosen test centre. Just call us on 0203 400 8738 and our team will take care of the rest.

3. Cancelling Your Booking

  • Cancellations made more than 48 hours before your scheduled test may be eligible for a full or partial refund, less any non-recoverable third-party fees
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours of your scheduled test may not be eligible for a refund, as test centre slots are reserved on your behalf
  • To cancel a booking, contact us as soon as possible on 0203 400 8738 or Info@constructionssupport.com

4. Non-Refundable Circumstances

  • Missed appointments without prior notice
  • Failed tests — a retake or new booking fee will apply for a further attempt
  • Card applications submitted to CITB, CSCS, JIB, ECS or CPCS on your behalf, where processing has already begun
  • Completed or partially completed e-learning courses

5. How To Request A Refund

To request a refund, contact our team by phone or email with your booking reference. We will confirm your eligibility and, where approved, process your refund to the original payment method.

6. Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–10 working days, though your bank or card provider may take longer to show the funds in your account.

7. Contact Us

For any questions about cancellations or refunds, call us on 0203 400 8738 or email Info@constructionssupport.com.

Construction Helps is your independent booking partner for CITB tests, CSCS, Skill and JIB cards, and online construction e-learning across the United Kingdom.
